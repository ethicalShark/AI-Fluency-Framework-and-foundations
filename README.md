# Claude AI Fluency & Foundations

## What is AI Fluency?
Developing practical skills, knowledge, insights, and values that reinforce each other and adapt as technology changes.

### 3 Ways to Engage with AI
- **Automation:** AI completes a specific task.
- **Augmentation:** You and AI work collaboratively (helps you do your work better).
- **Agency:** AI works independently on your behalf.

## 4D Framework

### 1. Delegation
Focuses on the big picture: AI vs. yourself.
- Problem awareness
- Platform awareness
- Task delegation

### 2. Description
Communicating clearly with AI.
- **Product:** What you want AI to create.
- **Process:** How AI should approach your request.
- **Performance:** How you want AI to behave during collaboration.

### 3. Discernment
Evaluating AI outputs. Description and Discernment work together through continuous feedback and improvement. Human judgment remains essential.

- **Product:** Quality, accuracy, and relevance.
- **Process:** How AI reaches its conclusions and whether its reasoning is logical.
- **Performance:** How effectively AI communicates and collaborates.

### 4. Diligence
Ensuring you interact with AI responsibly.

## Generative AI
AI generates content based on a prompt (e.g., an email).

### 3 Pillars
- **Algorithms**
- **Data:** Raw material for training.
- **Computation:** Computing power (clusters).

**Scaling Laws:** Increasing computing power, data, and model size generally improves AI capabilities.

### LLM Stages
1. **Pretraining:** Learning to predict.
2. **Fine-tuning:** Human feedback and reinforcement learning.
3. **Deployment:** Available for users to interact with.

### Capabilities
Versatility across language tasks, maintaining conversational flow, and switching between tasks without additional training.

### Limitations
Knowledge cutoffs, hallucinations, context window constraints, and reasoning challenges.

**Key takeaway:** The most effective applications combine the complementary strengths of humans and AI working together.

## Effective Prompting Techniques
- Provide context.
- Show examples of desired outputs.
- Specify constraints.
- Break complex tasks into steps.
- Ask AI to plan first.
- Define AI's role, tone, or style.

**The Secret Weapon:** Ask AI itself to help improve your prompt.
